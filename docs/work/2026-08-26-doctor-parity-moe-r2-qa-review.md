## 6. WHAT IS MISSING FROM THE PROOF

1. **A doctor run in every migration state, with its exit code.** P1 gives five: no row, closed row, open row, missing table, unreadable row — with Decision C (19/20 outrank 24), Decision D (`unreadable` ⇒ `Success`) and Decision E (a new status word). Required: (a) open row + healthy shape ⇒ line 9 open **and** `status: MIGRATION IN PROGRESS` **and** exit 24 **and** `ShouldNotContain("status: HEALTHY")`; (b) open row + shape-broken bank ⇒ exit **19**, `ShouldNotBe(24)`; (c) `DROP TABLE model_migration` ⇒ whatever the contract says **and** exit 0.
2. **The three lanes disagree on the missing-`model_migration`-table case and on the label.** P1 (line 9): guard tripped ⇒ `model migration: unreadable`, and `none open` only for closed/absent *row*.
