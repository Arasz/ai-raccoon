## Still open

- **F40:** production trigger rate not observed live (no live watch server run); the 2–6×10⁻⁵ rates are the harness's maximally tight race. Whether a `StopAsync` throw also skips the other hosted services' stops (generic-host aggregation) was not measured; the guaranteed skip of `StopAll`/`base.StopAsync` is what is claimed.
- **F5:** ORT 1.30.0 is *consistent with* the bit-drift but was not isolated as the cause (no 1.29 re-pin experiment run); x64 behaviour is derived from `runs-on` + `sameArchitecture`, not executed on x64.
- **F8:** whether a TRX written by the orphaned `nightly-triage.py` would record the discarded retry attempt is unverified — `--report-trx` on this runner produced "Zero tests ran", exit 5 (no TRX reporter), and no workflow generates a TRX. The repo's own status note says MTP drops xRetry's per-attempt messages in every flag combo.
- **F9:** the 26-vs-27 member counts are reflection for today and a name-list count for `155f281e` (old reflection not possible without an old build).
- **F10:** no harm demonstrated; the enforcement/read consequence (e.g. per-process vs per-bank divergence) is not measured live, only traced. ADR-0102 is the counter-argument and it is acknowledged in the finding.
